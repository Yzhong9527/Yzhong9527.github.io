Hi, I’m Yuanshan Zhong.

I am an M.S. student in Biomedical Engineering at the University at Buffalo, working as a Research Assistant in Prof. Wenyao Xu’s lab. I also conduct research on neurostimulation modeling in collaboration with Prof. Filip Stefanovic’s lab.

My research focuses on multimodal neural signal analysis, representation learning, and robust closed-loop neuroengineering systems. I am particularly interested in integrating sensing, modeling, and control for real-world neural systems. I also work on modeling and optimization of neurostimulation systems using finite-element simulations (COMSOL).

---

## Research Interests

- Multimodal neuroimaging (EEG, physiological signals)
- Neural signal representation learning
- Brain–computer interfaces
- Adaptive / closed-loop neural systems
- Neurostimulation modeling and optimization

---

## Selected Projects

### Multimodal Neural Signal Analysis for Auditory Disorders
Multimodal EEG + physiological signal analysis for objective biomarker discovery in sound sensitivity disorders.

### Hybrid Learning for Closed-Loop Neural Control
Building robust neural control systems from noisy EEG signals using representation learning and hybrid learning approaches.

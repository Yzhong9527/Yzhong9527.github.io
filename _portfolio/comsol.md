## Overview

This project explores high-density neurostimulation systems using finite-element modeling (COMSOL) and data-driven optimization methods. The goal is to understand how electrode geometry and stimulation parameters influence electric field distribution, tissue heating, and stimulation selectivity in multilayer biological tissue.

The current stage of the project focuses on physics-based simulation and safety analysis of high-density electrode arrays under different stimulation settings. Ongoing work includes larger-scale parameter optimization and data-driven electrode selection strategies for targeted neurostimulation.

---

## Modeling Setup and Electric Field Distribution

![Electric potential distribution during high-density neurostimulation](/images/comsol_field_distribution.png)

*Representative finite-element simulation of electric potential distribution across multilayer biological tissue during high-density neurostimulation.*

The simulation framework models multilayer biological tissue and compares conventional single-electrode stimulation with high-density grid electrode configurations. Electric potential distributions are analyzed to study spatial selectivity and current spread during stimulation.

## Thermal Response Analysis

![Thermal response under increasing stimulation current](/images/comsol_current_thermal_response.png)

*Temperature rise under increasing stimulation current amplitudes. Safety-related thermal thresholds are shown for reference.*

Parameter sweeps were performed to analyze the relationship between stimulation settings and tissue heating. Current amplitude showed a strong nonlinear effect on temperature rise, while grid-based stimulation maintained thermal behavior comparable to conventional electrode configurations under matched conditions.

## My Contributions

- Developed multilayer finite-element models of biological tissue for neurostimulation simulation.
- Simulated electric potential distribution and thermal effects during electrical stimulation.
- Evaluated high-density grid electrode designs and compared them with conventional single-electrode configurations.
- Analyzed the effects of stimulation parameters, including current amplitude, pulse width, frequency, and stimulation duration.
- Generated simulation datasets across multiple parameter sweeps and electrode configurations.
- Explored data-driven optimization strategies for electrode selection and stimulation targeting.

---

## Current Findings

- High-density grid electrode arrays can maintain thermal safety comparable to conventional stimulation configurations under appropriate parameter settings.
- Electrode geometry and stimulation parameters strongly influence both electric field distribution and thermal behavior.
- Physics-based simulation provides an effective framework for studying stimulation safety and selectivity in neuroengineering systems.

---

## Ongoing Work

Current work focuses on expanding the simulation framework toward larger electrode arrays, improved spatial selectivity analysis, and data-driven optimization of stimulation strategies for targeted neurostimulation applications.

---

## Takeaway

This project demonstrates how finite-element modeling and computational analysis can support the design of safe, scalable, and selective neurostimulation systems for future neuroengineering applications.

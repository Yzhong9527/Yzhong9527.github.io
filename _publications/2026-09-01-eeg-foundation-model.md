This work investigates the distinction between detecting decodable information in EEG foundation-model representations and establishing what that information actually represents.

The study examines decoding validity through selection-aware permutation testing, mechanistic controls, subject-identity removal, temporal and task generalization, cross-model analysis, and cohort and pipeline sensitivity.

**Manuscript in preparation — submission planned for early September 2026.**

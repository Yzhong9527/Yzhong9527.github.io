This study presents a comparative electrothermal analysis of conventional single electrodes and small-electrode grid configurations for HD-inspired peripheral transcutaneous neurostimulation. Using coupled electrical–thermal finite-element modeling, we systematically evaluated how electrode geometry and stimulation parameters influence current distribution and tissue heating.
